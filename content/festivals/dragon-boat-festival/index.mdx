# Dragon Boat Festival (端午节)

The Dragon Boat Festival, also known as Duanwu Festival, is a traditional
Chinese festival held on the 5th day of the 5th lunar month.

## History

The festival commemorates the death of Qu Yuan, a famous poet and minister of
the Chu state during the Warring States period.

## Traditions

- **Dragon boat racing** — Teams paddle long boats decorated as dragons.
- **Zongzi** — Glutinous rice wrapped in bamboo leaves.
- **Hanging mugwort** — Placed on doors to ward off evil spirits.
